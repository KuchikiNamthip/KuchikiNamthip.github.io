I’m incredibly honored and excited to be joining the panel discussion at **The 3rd Thailand Bioinformatics Research Network Conference (TBRN 2026)** here in Khon Kaen! 🧬✨

As bioinformatics, AI, and precision medicine continue to transform healthcare, the question of how we ensure our data and discoveries remain reproducible, ethical, and trustworthy is one that I care about deeply.

Sharing the stage with such esteemed veterans in the field is truly humbling. As someone who is always eager to learn and grow in this rapidly evolving space, I am so grateful for the opportunity to represent Genfosis Co., Ltd. and discuss standards and data governance alongside these experts.

**Our Panel Lineup:**

- 👤 Dr. Sissades Tongsima (BIOTEC)
- 👤 Dr. Yotsawat Pomyen (Chulabhorn Research Institute)
- 👤 Asst. Prof. Dr. Apinya Jusakul (Khon Kaen University)
- 👤 Khun Buppha Techapatharaporn (NSTDA)
- 👤 Dr. Krittiyabhorn Kongtanawanich, Pharm. D., Ph.D. (Head of Bioinformatics, Genfosis Co., Ltd.) — That's me! 👋
- 🎙️ Moderated by Dr. Apiwat Sangphukieo (Chiang Mai University)

If you are attending the conference this week (June 10–12) at The Heritage Grand Khon Kaen Hotel, please come by our session and say hi! Whether you are a researcher, clinician, or data scientist, I would love to connect and hear your thoughts on the future of responsible bioinformatics.

🎟 Registration & details: [tbrn.batthailand.org/2026](https://tbrn.batthailand.org/2026/)
